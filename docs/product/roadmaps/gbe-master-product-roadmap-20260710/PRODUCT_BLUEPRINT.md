## Product outcome

GBE operates one trustworthy platform behind its primary public website: staff sign in under exactly three fail-closed roles and work in a dedicated operator console with a product-owned design system; inventory arrives through governed CSV/XLSX and agent-assisted imports with mapping, normalization, duplicate detection, dry run, human approval, idempotent commits, and rollback at ~1,400-vehicle scale; vehicle images and AI-generated media flow through durable Supabase Storage behind canonical Payload Media with provenance, moderation, budgets, and reviewer approval — never auto-published; the primary homepage and later pages are built in Builder V2 on a single versioned block registry with draft/preview/publish/unpublish/rollback and concurrency protection from site_config as the sole source of truth; the modern GBE Storefront renders those contracts with accurate navigation, complete deterministic catalog filters/sorts, trust and conversion modules, real 404s, SEO, and measurable performance; visitors convert through GBE-owned lead capture and dealership/city-aware WhatsApp handoff with approved off-site links as supplemental routing; sales staff manage leads in an inbox with assignment, stages, and response SLAs; and dealers read trusted analytics — bounded sessions, attribution, bot filtering, idempotent events, and rollups — while every release passes a pinned-SHA cross-repository gate proven by Node 22 CI.

## Global risks

- The database is live production Supabase data (project bdnvgbdmqmbzemllqngs, 1,391 vehicles); every migration in this roadmap — roles enum, price, media, homepage/legacy-home retirement — must be rehearsed on the F019 staging infrastructure with archived rollback evidence and never run against production during build work
- The fail-open role fallback (src/access/roles.ts:38) is live now: any malformed or roleless authenticated account holds effective admin across every guarded endpoint — including import commit and the AI generation endpoint if its flag is enabled — until F001 lands
- F002 remains open: exact Postgres version, policies, grants, SECURITY DEFINER functions, forced-RLS state, Storage bucket restrictions, and all exact aggregates are unknown; the migrations that consume them (F001 mapping, F009-F011, F013 reconciliation) carry hidden risk until D06 closes the captures — but per the consultation this must not stall M01 baseline work
- The working tree is not reproducible: uncommitted modifications plus numerous untracked review artifacts and scripts sit on codex/admin-workflow-updates; F018's deliberate review/save/discard disposition is the first prerequisite of every later claim
- Two populated homepage models (site_config and the legacy home family) compete for truth in the live database; storefront or content work done before F013's reconciliation risks building on the wrong source
- Raw REST and GraphQL remain mounted for all collections; any access-rule mistake anywhere becomes an immediate public exposure, which is why every access feature requires negative-path tests
- Media bytes are non-durable on serverless hosting and the service-role sync script is an active second write path into Supabase Storage; a redeploy can silently lose newly uploaded images until F009 ships, and public launch acceptance is explicitly gated on F009/F010
- Public lead/analytics ingestion is spoofable now; all current dashboard metrics (408 live analytics rows, 300-row read cap) are untrusted until F006 and F036 land; smart ranks stay removed and exposure signals stay off until trusted rollups exist
- No CI, typecheck, or test suite exists; until F018/F003, every acceptance criterion in this roadmap is manually asserted rather than mechanically enforced — and the critical role migration must not be attempted before the baseline harness exists
- The cost-bearing AI generation endpoint has no rate limit, timeout, retry, idempotency, cost ledger, quota, or moderation; enabling ENABLE_AI_IMAGE_WIZARD for the team before F022/F030 land creates direct financial and stability exposure
- The import pipeline commits in one pass with no dry run, approval, idempotency, or rollback; a malformed or duplicate file at ~1,400-row scale mutates live inventory with no undo until F023 lands
- All Storefront behavior is carried forward, not verified; contract changes in F004/F012/F013/F025 could break the live website without F020's early drift checks and F017's pinned-SHA gate
- The operator experience is embedded in Payload Admin behind an 8,722-line global stylesheet; heavy new investment inside that boundary (beyond F016 containment) would be discarded by the F026/F027 console migration
- Multi-workstream breadth (console, storefront, AI, analytics) can starve the security-critical path; Fable implementation approval must remain one bounded layer at a time even where parallel design tracks start early
